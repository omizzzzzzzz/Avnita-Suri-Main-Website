The Suri Program

Participant Information, Disclaimer and Terms & Conditions

Important Information Before Joining the Programme

This programme provides education, coaching and group support for people interested in exploring mind-body approaches to chronic pain and persistent symptoms.

The programme is not medical care, psychotherapy, physiotherapy, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for advice from your GP, consultant, pain clinic, therapist or other qualified healthcare professional.

By joining the programme, you understand and agree that:

  • The programme is educational and supportive in nature.
  • No diagnosis will be made during the programme.
  • No individual medical advice will be given.
  • You remain responsible for your own health decisions.
  • You should not stop, reduce or change any medication, treatment, exercise plan or medical care without first speaking to an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
  • Participation is voluntary, and you should only take part in exercises or reflections that feel safe and appropriate for you.
  • Results vary from person to person, and no specific outcome, improvement, reduction in pain, or recovery is promised or guaranteed.

The programme may include education about chronic pain, the nervous system, stress, emotions, fear, attention, self-reflection, journaling, gentle awareness practices and behavioural tools. Some people may find this material emotionally activating, challenging or uncomfortable at times. You are encouraged to go at your own pace, pause when needed, and seek appropriate professional support if difficult feelings arise.

Medical Responsibility

You acknowledge that persistent symptoms can sometimes be associated with underlying medical conditions. Participation in this programme is not intended to replace appropriate medical assessment. You confirm that you have consulted a healthcare professional where appropriate regarding your symptoms and will continue to seek medical advice for any concerns about your health.

This programme is not suitable as a replacement for urgent or specialist medical assessment. You should seek medical advice promptly if you have new, severe, unexplained or rapidly worsening symptoms, or if you are concerned about your health. In an emergency, contact emergency services or attend A&E.

Crisis Support

The programme does not provide crisis support. If you are experiencing thoughts of harming yourself or others, feel unsafe, or are in immediate distress, please contact emergency services, NHS 111, your GP, a crisis helpline, or your local urgent mental health support service.

Testimonials and Personal Stories

Any examples, testimonials, case studies or personal recovery stories shared during the programme are provided for educational and illustrative purposes only. They do not constitute a promise, prediction or guarantee that any participant will achieve similar results.

Group Participation and Confidentiality

Because this is a group programme, participants are expected to respect each other's privacy. You agree not to record sessions, share other participants' personal information, or discuss identifiable details outside the group. While reasonable steps are taken to create a respectful and confidential space, absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in a group setting.

Online Participation

Online participation involves some risks, including technical disruption, privacy risks, or the possibility that others in your environment may overhear. You are responsible for joining from a private and safe space where possible.

Programme materials, recordings, worksheets and educational content are provided for personal use only and may not be copied, distributed, reproduced, sold or shared without written permission.
